The case of Diego's essay

Diego is a first-year university student. English is his second language. For his English class, he submitted a 1,200-word essay called "The impact of social media on teenagers".

The school ran the essay through an AI detector. The result: 87% likely written by AI. His teacher says the essay is "too perfect" compared to his earlier work. Diego says he wrote it himself and only used ChatGPT to fix his grammar.

The question for this hearing
